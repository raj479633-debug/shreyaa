Haute Bridal Atelier • Darbhanga, Bihar

Every bride carries centuries of heritage in her attire. Her makeup must honor that weight with lightness.

Founded by master artist Shreya Chaudhary, Shreya Makeovers was conceived to liberate bridal beauty from heavy, uniform layers. We believe in an editorial, skin-first approach where micro-hydration and shade-matching take precedence over cover-up pigments.

Each look is customized through lighting colorimetry—accounting for the exact gold karat of your polki, the ambient warmth of temple diyas or pheras, and the high-resolution sensors of modern cinematography. The result is a luminous, weightless presence that remains impeccable from dawn until late-night farewells.
